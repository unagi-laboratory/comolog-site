Comolog Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 18, 2026
Last updated: October 21, 2026

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If there is any difference between this version and the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

Comolog (the "App") is a study and work time tracker that automatically records the time you spend at places. It is developed and operated by Unagi Laboratory (the "Developer"). This policy explains what information the App handles and why.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Location (including in the background)

The App uses location through the operating system's geofencing feature to notice when you arrive at or leave the places you've added (there is a limit to how many places you can add). To keep recording while the App is closed, the App asks you to set location access to "Allow all the time."

  • The only location information stored is the coordinates of the places you've added and the sessions you spent there.
  • The App never records the routes you travel.
  • The App does not use GPS continuously. To notice arrivals and departures, it uses the operating system's geofencing feature, which keeps battery use low.
  • Only when the App detects that you've entered one of your places, it checks your current location once so that it doesn't record you by mistake when you're in a nearby building. This location is not stored.
  • If that check shows you're still outside the place, the App keeps that spot as a "marker" only on your phone and checks again when you move away from it. The marker never leaves your phone (it isn't included in device backups either) and is deleted automatically once it's no longer needed.
  • On the screen for adding a place, the App gets your current location temporarily to center the map. This location is not stored.

1.2 Places you add

The name, coordinates, radius, color, and icon of each place. Only places you add yourself are stored.

1.3 Sessions

Which place you were at, and from when to when (start time, end time, and duration). The same applies to sessions you record manually.

1.4 Account information

  • When you first open the App, an anonymous account (a random user ID) is created automatically. You don't need to enter your name or anything else.
  • To back up your sessions and restore them on another device, you can link a Google account if you wish. If you do, profile information received from Google, such as your email address and name, is stored in Firebase Authentication.

1.5 Settings

Your settings in the App, such as the minimum stay and whether notifications are on or off.

1.6 Purchase information (Comolog Supporter Edition)

Comolog Supporter Edition is purchased through Google Play's payment system.

  • To confirm whether you've made a purchase and to restore it when you change phones or reinstall the App, we collect your purchase history (the product, date and time, Google Play order number, and so on), linked to your user ID.
  • Payment information such as your credit card number is handled by Google Play. The Developer never receives your payment information.

1.7 Contact information

If you contact us through "Contact us" in the App, we receive your email address, your name (optional), and your message through the email sent from your email app.

1.8 Information we don't collect

The App does not collect your routes, contacts, photos, advertising ID, or information about other apps on your device. The App shows no ads and does not use analytics that track your behavior (for crash and error reports, see 1.9).

1.9 Information for improving the App (crash and error reports)

When the App closes unexpectedly or a connection error occurs, the App automatically sends information about it through Firebase Crashlytics (what went wrong and where, your device model and OS version, the App version, and an anonymous identifier) so that we can find the cause and fix it. It does not send what you entered or did in the App (such as search terms or your location).

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect only for the following purposes:

  1. Recording your sessions automatically and showing them as charts and history
  2. Syncing your sessions across devices and restoring them when you change phones
  3. Showing notifications when a session starts or ends (only if you turn them on) and when a session's end time is unknown (all of these are local notifications created on your device)
  4. Confirming purchases of Comolog Supporter Edition and restoring them when you change phones or reinstall the App
  5. Investigating problems and improving the App
  6. Replying to your inquiries

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not provide or sell the information we collect to any third party, except as required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Your data is kept until you delete it.

5.1 Deleting your sessions

  • In the App, go to Settings → Manage sessions → Delete all sessions to delete all your places, sessions, and settings at any time, including those stored on our servers.
  • You can delete individual sessions from History at any time.

5.2 Deleting your account

  • If you've linked a Google account, go to Settings → Account → Delete account in the App to delete your account along with your places, sessions, and settings.
  • If you can't use the App, contact us at the address below with the subject "Delete my Comolog account" and include the email address of the Google account you linked. After confirming your identity, we will delete your account and sessions.

5.3 Information that remains after deletion

  • Purchase history: Because it's needed to restore purchases, it remains with RevenueCat and Google Play even after you delete your sessions or account. To ask for it to be deleted, contact us at the address below (after deletion, you may no longer be able to restore your purchase).
  • Crash and error reports: Kept in Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Emails you send us: Kept in the Developer's mailbox as a record of our replies. Let us know if you'd like them deleted.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page. If the changes are significant, we will also let you know in the App.
